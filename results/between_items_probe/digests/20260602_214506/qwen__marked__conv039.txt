Capacity concerns (disk usage) on regions like `prod-west-1`, `staging-east-1`, and `analytics-east-1` lead to additional provisioning and scheduled vacuums.

*   **Compliance & Security:** Ongoing security scans revealing medium-severity items (often related to pagination cursors, connection pools, or cert issues), SOC2 audit preparation, GDPR data deletion decisions, and PCI scope constraints on certain branches.
